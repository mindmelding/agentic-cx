# Account health check-in

## When this is the moment

Row 4 shows declining usage, row 2 shows silence longer than their usual pattern, or row 5 shows a drop in a key metric. Or you're running the weekly review and an account is drifting from its stated outcome. This is always proactive, never reactive to their message.

## What the best person on the floor does

Checks in with specifics, not "how's it going." Names what they see in the data, asks one concrete question about it, offers one path forward. Makes it a conversation, not a survey. If the account is healthy, the check-in is the 5% moment: acknowledges progress and asks what changed. Never checks in to check a box.

## What an ordinary company does

Sends a "we haven't heard from you" template. Asks "is everything okay?" with no data. Schedules a quarterly business review that's a sales pitch. Waits until renewal to notice they stopped using the product.

## Steps

1. Read rows 2, 4, 5. Confirm the signal: usage down, key feature unused, stated outcome stalled, or silence after regular contact.
2. Pull the specific data: last login, feature usage this month vs. last, error rate, or the milestone they haven't hit.
3. Reach out with the data first, the question second. "I noticed X, wondering if Y."
4. Offer one concrete next step: a call, a config review, turning on a feature, or asking what changed in their world.
5. If they're healthy and ahead: say what you see and ask what they're doing differently. That becomes a learned pattern for other accounts.
6. Write back: what you saw, what they said, updated health signal, next action with date.

## Guardrails specific to this moment

Reading detailed usage or account data may need a nudge if it's the first time or crosses into row 10. Never open with "we miss you" or "we noticed you haven't logged in." State the data, not the emotion. Check-ins on healthy accounts should be rare; they're earned by tight work on the 95%.

## Good / Bad example

**Bad.** "Hi! We noticed it's been a while since you logged in. Just checking in to see if everything is okay with your account. Let us know if you need any help!"

**Good.** "Hi Maya. Your team ran 40 imports in August and 3 in September, and the weekly report hasn't been opened in two weeks. Wondering if priorities shifted, or if something broke on our side that I should fix."

## The 5% version

For a healthy account: notice the pattern that's working and offer to replicate it. "You've cut support volume by half since July. I see you built that onboarding workflow; want me to document it so your next hire can copy it?"

## Write back

Account health signal (up/down/stable), usage trend with numbers, what prompted the check-in, their response, next action with owner and date.

## Evals

- Must state specific data in the opening (a number, a date, a pattern).
- Must not ask "how's it going" or any vague wellness check.
- Must offer one concrete path forward.
