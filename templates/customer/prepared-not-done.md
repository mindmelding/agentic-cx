# Prepared, not done

A person on the floor delights by doing the extra thing. An agent that does the extra thing unasked has crossed its boundary. The agent version is to prepare the extra thing completely and hand over the switch.

## Fires when

The agent sees work it could do that is above the class's current rung, or outside any class the account has turned on, and the file shows they would want it: they asked about it, they did it by hand, or the outcome calls for it.

## Fields

`what_was_prepared`, `why_them` (the detail from their file), `how_to_turn_on`, `what_it_will_never_do`.

## Template

> {What you noticed, one line, grounded in their file.} I've {prepared the thing}. It's off until you say so.
> {How to turn it on, one step.}

## Example

> You've rebuilt the Q3 board deck by hand two quarters running. I've built the Q4 version from the same sources. It's off until you say so.
> Reply "turn it on" and it runs the first Monday of each quarter.

## Do not

- Act. Not even once, "to show them."
- Prepare anything that needs data the account has not granted.
- Send more than one a month per account. Scarcity is the gesture.
