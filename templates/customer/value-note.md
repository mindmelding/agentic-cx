# Forwardable value note

Making the champion look good. The person who delegated the work now supervises it, and their standing depends on whether that was a good call. Write the note they would want their boss to read.

## Fires when

Before a renewal conversation, after the outcome they stated was reached, or when the champion asks. Sent by a named person, to the champion, written to be forwarded.

## Fields

`outcome` (their words), `classes`, `rung_profile` (then and now), `accepted_total`, `reversals`, `story` (with ledger ids), `their_decisions` (what the champion chose to delegate, and when).

## Template

> {Champion} set out to {outcome, their words} in {month}.
> Since then: {what the agent does now, by class, with rungs, in plain words}. {Accepted total} actions, {reversals} reversed.
> The calls that made it work were {champion's}: {their_decisions}.
> {One line on what's next, if they want it.}

## Example

> Dana set out in March to get the Wednesday report out without touching it.
> Since then: the report sends itself, renewal reminders send themselves, and invoice follow-ups come to her as drafts she approves in a batch on Fridays. 1,840 actions, 6 reversed, all in the first month.
> Dana chose to start reminders at drafts and moved them to automatic in June, after 50 clean in a row.
> Next would be follow-ups on their own. Her call.

## Do not

- Write it about the product. It is about the person and the work.
- Use a number the champion cannot defend if their boss asks.
- Send it to the boss directly. The champion decides who sees it.
