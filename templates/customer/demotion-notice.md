# Demotion notice

A rung dropped. Said plainly, with the way back. Silence after a demotion reads as hiding.

## Fires when

A rung drops for any reason: a reversal, a broken `never`, a forensic review, or the customer asked. Sent with the incident note when there is one, alone when there is not.

## Fields

`class`, `rung_was`, `rung_now`, `why`, `what_it_takes_to_return`, `owner`.

## Template

> From {when}, {class in plain words} {what rung_now means: drafts for your review / proposals only}. {Why, one sentence.}
> {What it takes to go back: the fix, and the record we'll watch.} {Owner} will tell you when it qualifies.

## Example

> From today, invoice follow-ups come to you as drafts again. One went to the wrong contact on Monday, and we want you to see each one until the fix is proven.
> Once the fix ships, 25 clean drafts in a row puts it back. I'll tell you when it qualifies. — Priya

## Do not

- Demote silently.
- Restore silently. The return is its own message: a [promotion offer](promotion-offer.md).
