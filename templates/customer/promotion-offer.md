# Promotion offer

The next rung, offered as a gift. Time back, on their own record, and theirs to refuse. This is the brief from [change](../../responsibilities/change.md), written for the person who has to say yes.

## Fires when

A class on an account meets the promotion bar (by default 50 consecutive instances, acceptance at or above 95 percent, no boundary violations). The system proposes. A person on the team approves the send. The customer applies it.

## Fields

`class`, `rung_now`, `rung_next`, `instances`, `accepted`, `edited`, `reversed`, `never_checks`, `what_changes`, `how_to_undo`.

## Template

> You haven't changed one of {these} in {period}: {accepted} of the last {instances} went out exactly as drafted.
> Want me to {what rung_next does, in plain words} from now on? {What stays the same: the never lines that still hold.}
> You can switch it back {how_to_undo}. I'll tell you how it went after {first check-in}.

## Example

> You haven't changed one of the renewal reminders in six weeks: 52 of the last 52 went out as drafted.
> Want me to send them without waiting for you? I'll still never send one to an account marked in dispute, and you'll see each one in the Thursday list.
> You can switch it back from Settings, or just tell me. I'll check in after the first ten.

## Do not

- Offer a rung for a class that demoted in the last 30 days.
- Bundle two promotions in one message.
- Phrase it as an upsell. If the next rung costs money, a person sends it and says so plainly.
