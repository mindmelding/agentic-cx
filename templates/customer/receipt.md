# Receipt

The better the agent works, the less the customer sees it. The receipt makes the work visible once, at a moment it means something, in the terms of what they wanted.

## Fires when

One of these, at most once per account per two weeks:

- A class ran a full cycle on its own for the first time (the first week at rung 3 with no reversals).
- A milestone on the desired outcome: the fourth Wednesday report, the hundredth invoice chased.
- A quiet week after a hard one. The record shows it settled.

Never on a calendar alone. A weekly digest nobody asked for is a newsletter.

## Fields

`outcome` (their words), `class`, `count`, `accepted`, `edited`, `reversed`, `period`, `time_saved` only if the customer told you the before.

## Template

> {Outcome, in their words} happened again{, on its own,} {period}: {count} {things}, {accepted} went out as written{, {edited} you changed}.
> {One line on what that means for them, using something they told you.}
> {Optional: one decision. Usually none.}

## Example

> The Wednesday report went out on its own for the fourth week running. 4 of 4 sent as drafted, no edits.
> You said in March this was the thing eating your Tuesday nights. It hasn't been opened in the editor since.

## Do not

- Report a total you cannot open row by row.
- Claim time saved that the customer never told you it used to take.
- Send it the week after an incident. Recovery first.
