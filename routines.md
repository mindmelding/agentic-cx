# Routines

One queue, worked by one loop. The queue and how it learns are in [`queue.md`](queue.md). The floor canon in [`floor/`](floor/README.md) is how a customer moment is handled. These skills are when, and what is kept.

Company notes stay in `local/`. Copy that folder to move them to another machine or another agent. The public manual stays free of one company's customers.

## First

[Setup](skill/SKILL.md), once. It ends with a plan of setup items in the queue. You work them the same way as every day after.

## Start of day

About ten minutes. [Open](skills/open/SKILL.md).

It reads what is new, turns each thing into an item, carries out what is allowed to run on its own, and posts the top five. It can run on a schedule before you arrive.

## Throughout the day

[Triage](skills/triage/SKILL.md). Top item first: it loads the right playbook or page, drafts or does the work at the item's rung, asks at most one thing, and logs what you did.

When a customer pings you directly, [floor](skills/floor/SKILL.md) is faster. It still logs the moment.

## End of day

About ten minutes. [Close](skills/close/SKILL.md).

The day note, and what carries over. On Friday, close also reads the week's log: it proposes rung promotions for kinds of work you keep accepting unedited, rules from edits you keep making, and narrower producers for items you keep dropping. Then the autonomy report. That is the whole weekly review.

## Monthly

About thirty minutes. [Refresh](skills/refresh/SKILL.md).

Re-verify the harness tables in [`enablement/`](enablement/README.md) and the standards in [`agent-customers.md`](agent-customers.md). What cannot be verified now becomes an `upkeep` item.
